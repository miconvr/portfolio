Kaltas live

Nov 2026 to Present

Kaltas is a privacy-first Chrome extension that shows students the discounts and education perks available on the website they are visiting. It matches the current site locally against a curated offer list, with a focus on students in the Philippines and global offers included.

I designed, built, and packaged it on my own, from the offer matching and popup interface to the Chrome Web Store submission. It is open source under the MIT License.

CivicGuard in development

Sep 2026 to Present

CivicGuard is an AI-integrated incident and curfew monitoring system built as our capstone project at Our Lady of Fatima University. It connects residents with barangay staff through reporting, status updates, and incident management tools.

I led our four-member team as Project Manager and Full-Stack Developer. The project is being developed with Franz Mikey Reyes, Carl Spencer Talon, and Adriann Enriquez.
