CivicGuard in development

A full-stack web system for Barangay Maimpis in San Fernando, Pampanga. Residents report an incident with a category, description, location and an optional photo and GPS pin, then follow it from Received to Resolved and can confirm a fix or reopen it within 7 days. Staff work from an urgency-sorted queue. Residents get a notification when the status of a report changes, and can install the app on their phone from the browser.

A chatbot helps residents write reports and gives emergency guidance in English and Tagalog, and AI suggests a category and urgency that staff can always change. Access is role-based for residents, tanods, officials and admins, with audit logs, analytics and PDF exports.

Developed by Mico Gerard Navarro, Franz Mikey Reyes, Carl Spencer Talon and Adriann Enriquez.
