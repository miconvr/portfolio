The problem

Residents of Barangay Maimpis in San Fernando, Pampanga needed one place to report an incident and follow what happens to it. Barangay staff needed one place to review, assign and resolve reports, and to log curfew violations.

What I did

I’m building CivicGuard as a full-stack web system with two sides.

For residents: report an incident with a category, description, location, and an optional photo and GPS pin. Follow the report from Received to Resolved, and confirm a fix or reopen it within 7 days.

For staff: work from an urgency-sorted queue. Access is role-based for residents, tanods, officials and admins, with audit logs, analytics and PDF exports.

AI assistance: a chatbot helps residents write reports and gives emergency guidance in English and Tagalog. AI also suggests a category and urgency, and staff can always change it.

What came of it

CivicGuard is still in development. The current version is live with role-based access, English and Tagalog support, an installable mobile experience, and a history of more than 250 commits.
