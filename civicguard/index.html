The project

CivicGuard is our capstone project at Our Lady of Fatima University. It was designed to improve incident reporting and curfew monitoring in Barangay Maimpis by connecting residents with barangay staff through one web platform.

Residents can submit reports, track updates, and follow what happens next. Staff can review, assign, update, and manage incidents from the same system.

My role

I led a four-member team as Project Manager and Full-Stack Developer, coordinating the project while building major parts of the application.

I developed resident reporting and follow-up features with optional photo attachments and location pins. I also implemented staff workflows for report review, assignment, status updates, residence verification, curfew incident logging, and analytics.

The AI-assisted reporting features use Google Gemini and Groq to help residents write reports and classify them. Staff retain responsibility for reviewing reports and making decisions.

I also worked on English and Tagalog support, role-based access control, and automated testing through GitHub Actions CI.

What came of it

CivicGuard is still in development and is deployed on Wasmer. The current version brings resident reporting, staff management, AI-assisted writing and classification, multilingual support, role-based access, and testing into one capstone project.
