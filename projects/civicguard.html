CivicGuard

1 / 3

CivicGuard is a capstone project at Our Lady of Fatima University that improves incident reporting and curfew monitoring in Barangay Maimpis. Residents submit and track reports, and barangay staff review and manage them. AI helps residents describe incidents, while staff make the final decisions.

Built with: Franz Mikey Reyes, Carl Spencer Talon, and Adriann Enriquez.
