About

I’m a developer based in Pampanga, Philippines. I like projects where a real person has a real job to get done, whether that’s a barangay official reviewing reports or someone walking a daily round.

My recent work focuses on accessibility. I write components from scratch against the WAI-ARIA patterns, test them using only the keyboard, and compare them with libraries like shadcn/ui and Radix to learn what the details are for.

I also use AI assistants heavily, and I treat their output as a draft that needs checking.
